Privacy Policy

KingdomCore – CrownOS Kingshot Companion

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KingdomCore, also known as CrownOS, handles information when users interact with the application through Discord.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, KingdomCore may process the following categories of information:

  • Discord information: Discord user IDs, usernames, server IDs, channel IDs, and other information made available through Discord interactions.
  • Game-related information: Kingshot player identifiers, governor names, kingdom information, alliance details, rankings, player statistics, and recruitment-related information.
  • Command and interaction information: Information submitted when users execute bot commands or use application features.
  • Operational information: Technical logs, error information, timestamps, and records needed to operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide player lookup and kingdom analytics.
  • Support alliance management and recruitment scouting.
  • Provide gift-code and game-related utilities.
  • Maintain application functionality and databases.
  • Investigate errors, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

4. Storage and Retention

KingdomCore uses application databases and local storage for operational records. Some information may be retained to support historical statistics, scouting results, alliance features, and application functionality.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for operational purposes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

KingdomCore does not intentionally sell personal information to third parties.

The application operates through Discord and may interact with external game-related services or data sources. Information processed by those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the application and its users.

6. Public and Game-Related Information

Some player, kingdom, alliance, and game-related information may come from publicly accessible sources, game-related services, or information provided by users. Displayed information may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application data and operational systems. However, no internet-connected service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. User Requests

For questions about information associated with your use of KingdomCore, or to request review or deletion of information where applicable, contact the project maintainers through the GitHub repository.

Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons.

9. Children's Privacy

KingdomCore is not intentionally designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users should follow the applicable age requirements and terms of Discord and other services they use.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be published at this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the project maintainers through the KingdomCore GitHub repository.
